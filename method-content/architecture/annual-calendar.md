# Premise

The Folicelle Method runs January to December. Members move through the year together as a community, even though the eight core modules are watched at each member's own pace. Dafina is consistently present at exactly two moments per year: the January kickoff week and the December reflection. Everything in between is light-touch, async, community-driven.

This is the only calendar in the system. There are no 30-day timers. There are no rolling 90-day windows tied to individual purchase dates. The shared annual rhythm is the moat.

---

# January, Kickoff Week (annual)

Target dates Year 1: Monday January 6 through Sunday January 12, 2026. Adjusted year over year to land on a working Monday in early January.

**Monday (Day 1)**: All eight core modules unlock for every enrolled member. Returning Year 1 members are reset into Year 2 community automatically. New members see the Baseline Assessment as their first action, gating Module 1 unlock until completed.

User action: complete Baseline Assessment (3 to 5 minutes), receive cohort assignment, then Module 1 is available.

Brand touchpoint: Dafina welcome email to all enrolled members from a personal-feeling sender ("Dafina at Folicelle"). One short paragraph. Includes the live Friday kickoff calendar invite.

Community: cross-cohort thread opens. Prompt: "What brought you to the Method this year." Dafina posts the first reply.

**Tuesday (Day 2)**: Dafina posts one short message in the community. The Method is a year, not a 30-day program. What that actually means. No timeline pressure.

**Wednesday (Day 3)**: Dafina posts one short message in the community. The four cohorts and how to use them.

**Thursday (Day 4)**: Dafina posts one short message in the community. How the Doctor Script Generator and the Toolkit work together.

**Friday (Day 5)**: Live evening Dafina kickoff. 45 to 60 minutes. Held on Zoom, streamed into the community as embedded video. Format: the origin of the Method (the ferritin-7 story), what to expect this year, how to use the Toolkit and the Doctor Script Generator, an open Q&A. Recording posted to the December Reflection section within 48 hours for members who miss the live.

Brand touchpoint: post-event email recap to all members with a one-line summary and the recording link.

**Weekend (Days 6 and 7)**: Members watch Module 1 and Module 2 at their pace. No prompts from Dafina. Community remains open for peer conversation.

# February, the room to actually watch

User action: members are encouraged to finish Modules 1 through 3 by the end of February. No deadline, no shaming. Reminder email week one of February.

Brand touchpoint: one community moderator-led prompt mid-month inviting members to share an unexpected observation from the first three modules. Dafina is not in the community this month.

---

# March, the month to start ordering labs

User action: members who finished Module 4 use the Doctor Script Generator to print their personalized script. They book the appointment, schedule the labs.

Brand touchpoint: Klaviyo email "If your labs are still on your to-do list" sent to members who have not generated a Doctor Script yet. Soft tone, no pressure. Same email a week later to non-openers, no further.

Community moderator opens a thread: "Booking your first appointment. What's getting in the way."

---

# April, Quarterly Bonus Drop 1 and the first check-in

Target drop date: Tuesday April 14, 2026 (and the second Tuesday of April every year).

**Week of April 7 (Check-in week, leading into the drop)**:
User action: members complete the Q1 check-in. Three components, all async, all in the Toolkit.
1. Upload three-angle photo to the Photo Tracker. Private by default.
2. Write a short journal entry. Prompt: "What has shifted, what hasn't, what are you carrying into the next quarter." Free text. Optionally shareable to the cohort.
3. Optionally share the check-in in the cohort community thread for peer support.

Brand touchpoint: scheduled Klaviyo email April 7 with the check-in prompt. Sender voice: Dafina, brief, warm, "you're doing this." Sent again April 11 to members who have not completed the check-in.

Community: the four cohort channels each open a check-in thread. Members reply to each other. Moderator presence, no Dafina presence.

**Tuesday April 14, Bonus Drop 1**:
The April bonus module releases. Year 1 topic: **The Heat, Water, and Topicals Deep Dive**. 8 to 12 minutes. Founder-taught.

Brand touchpoint: Klaviyo email day of release. Subject line voice: "What I changed about my shower water." Body links to the new module.

---

# May, the month to act on labs

User action: members whose labs came back in April begin discussing results with their providers, enter results into the Lab Results Module of the Toolkit.

Brand touchpoint: one Klaviyo email mid-month "If your results came back and you're not sure what they mean." Links to the Lab Results Module and to the cohort thread for peer interpretation.

Community: moderator-led thread "What did your labs tell you." No Dafina presence.

---

# June, the natural midpoint

User action: members who have not yet started Modules 5 or 6 receive a soft nudge. Members in the cohort threads keep talking to each other.

Brand touchpoint: a single community-driven thread, opened by the moderator. "Where are you in the Method? What do you want from the second half of the year." Members reply. Moderator highlights themes. No Dafina presence.

---

# July, Quarterly Bonus Drop 2 and the second check-in

Target drop date: Tuesday July 14, 2026.

**Week of July 7 (Check-in week)**: same three-component check-in pattern as April. Photo upload, journal entry, cohort thread. Klaviyo email July 7 and July 11.

**Tuesday July 14, Bonus Drop 2**:
The July bonus module releases. Year 1 topic: **The HRT, GLP-1, and Hair Loss Specifics Module**. 8 to 12 minutes. Founder-taught. Reviewed by Dr. Safahi before release.

Brand touchpoint: Klaviyo email day of release. Subject line voice: "If you're on GLP-1 or HRT, this one is for you."

---

# August, peak summer, low touch

User action: members continue at their pace. Many members are away from screens. Honor that.

Brand touchpoint: nothing from Folicelle in August except the transactional emails (renewal reminders, shipping). Community remains open, moderator present.

---

# September, back to school, back to the framework

User action: members re-engage with the Method as the year resumes. Members who paused over August come back to community.

Brand touchpoint: one Klaviyo email mid-September from Dafina, two paragraphs. "Where I am in my own framework right now." Personal, not promotional. Drives re-engagement.

Community: cross-cohort thread "Where are you returning to. What do you want from the last quarter."

---

# October, Quarterly Bonus Drop 3 and the third check-in

Target drop date: Tuesday October 13, 2026.

**Week of October 6 (Check-in week)**: same three-component check-in pattern. Klaviyo email October 6 and October 10.

**Tuesday October 13, Bonus Drop 3**:
The October bonus module releases. Year 1 topic: **The Hairstylist Relationship Module**. 8 to 12 minutes. Founder-taught. Maps cleanly to the sister-brand narrative with Sitting Pretty.

Brand touchpoint: Klaviyo email day of release. Sister-brand visibility (Sitting Pretty stylist network referenced in the email body where appropriate, without conflating the brands).

---

# November, the month to finish and to write

User action: members are encouraged to finish Modules 7 and 8 by mid-November, in advance of the December reflection. The Course-Completion Re-assessment in Module 8 should be submitted by November 30 for the member to be acknowledged in the December reflection.

Brand touchpoint: Klaviyo email week one of November "The reflection is December 11. Here's what to do between now and then."

Community: moderator opens a thread inviting members to share a sentence about what the Method changed for them. Submissions feed the December reflection narrative.

---

# December, the Reflection

Target dates Year 1: live reflection event on Thursday December 10, 2026. From Year 2 onward, target the second Thursday in December.

**Week of December 6 (Reflection week)**:
User action: complete the year. Watch any unfinished modules. Submit the Course-Completion Re-assessment if not already submitted.

Brand touchpoint: a Klaviyo email December 6 with the live reflection calendar invite and the recording promise for members who cannot attend.

**Thursday December 10, Live Reflection (Year 1)**:
45 to 60 minutes. Held on Zoom, streamed into the community as embedded video. Dafina hosts. Format: reflection on the year, themes from the journal prompts and community threads, three to five member stories shared with explicit permission, a preview of Year 2 bonus modules, a personal note from Dafina. Recording posted to the December Reflection section within 48 hours.

Brand touchpoint: post-event email recap. Personal, brief.

**Week of December 13 to December 31**:
User action: members complete the year at their own pace. Members renewing for Year 2 receive their renewal confirmation.

Brand touchpoint: light. One end-of-year community thread inviting members to share what they want to bring into Year 2. Moderator runs it.

---

# January Year 2, the reset

Target dates: Monday January 5 through Sunday January 11, 2027.

User action: every member resets. Returning members are auto-routed into Year 2 community. The eight core modules remain available with any updates Dafina has recorded over the prior year. The three Year 1 bonus modules (April, July, October) remain in the library, accessible to all members. The Baseline Assessment is offered again as a "Year 2 snapshot" for members who want to retake it.

Brand touchpoint: a new kickoff week, same rhythm as Year 1. Dafina's January 9 Friday evening live event opens the new year.

Bonus module Year 2 candidates (TBC by Dafina in November Year 1): Post-Thyroid, Post-Pill, Pregnancy and Postpartum Deep Dive, Iron Absorption Deep Dive, Functional Medicine 101, Reading Your Own Cycle.

# Operating notes

- All scheduled emails sit in Klaviyo and route through the existing brand sender identity.
- All community posts route through Tevello's native community feature.
- All live events use Zoom for production and embed recordings in Tevello.
- The community moderator works ~5 to 10 hours per week (per master handoff Section 1). The cadence above keeps moderator load roughly flat across the year.
- The first complete year of this calendar (2026) is the test. November Year 1 includes a review of what worked, what to cut, what to add for 2027.
