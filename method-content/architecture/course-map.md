# Overview

The Folicelle Method is a founder-fronted course on hair wellness, built around the framework Dafina wished she had when her ferritin came back at 7. It is taught by Dafina in her own voice, with clinical interview cut-ins from Dr. Miriam Safahi, MD inside the Hormonal and Nutritional modules. Foundation is the daily ritual that pairs with the framework, not the subject of the course.

The Method runs on an annual January to December rhythm. All eight core modules unlock on day one of the January kickoff week. Members watch at their own pace through the year. Three quarterly bonus modules drop in April, July, and October. The year closes with a live December reflection. Members reset into Year 2 every January.

Two entry points, one course:

1. **Foundation subscriber.** Course access is included with the subscription (90-day prepaid, monthly, or annual). Community access is included. Toolkit access is included.
2. **Standalone Method buyer.** $79 one-time grants lifetime course access, 30-day community trial, full Toolkit access, Care Network awareness. Upgrade to Foundation later credits the $79.

# Kickoff week (annual, runs roughly January 6 to January 12)

The kickoff is the only week of the year Dafina is consistently present in the community. Energy and accountability are highest here, and the architecture should match.

**Monday, January 6.** All eight core modules unlock for every member, new or returning. Welcome email to all enrolled members from Dafina. Community thread opens: "What brought you to the Method this year." Existing Year 1 members reset into Year 2 community automatically. New members complete the in-course Baseline Assessment before Module 1 unlocks.

**Tuesday through Thursday.** Dafina posts one short message per day in the community: a question, a piece of context, a recommendation. Async, not live. These are designed to anchor the cohort and seed early threads.

**Friday, January 10.** Live evening Dafina kickoff, 45 to 60 minutes. Held on Zoom, streamed into the community via embedded video. Recording stored in the course for members who miss it. Content: the origin of the Method, what to expect this year, how the assessment, the doctor script, and the bonus modules will work, an open Q&A.

**Weekend.** Members watch Module 1 and Module 2 at their pace.

# The eight core modules

All eight unlock simultaneously on day one of kickoff week. Members are encouraged but not required to watch in order. Length is variable per the subject. Short where the subject is contained, longer where it is the spine of the framework.

## Module 1, How Hair Actually Works

**Length: 12 to 15 minutes.** Taught by Dafina.

The biology beneath the surface. How a follicle is built, why hair grows in cycles, and the lifecycle most of us were never taught. Inside this module, Dafina walks through the heritage of He Shou Wu, the thousand-year-old Eastern botanical at the heart of Foundation, and why she chose it as the anchor ingredient. The He Shou Wu story sits inside Module 1 rather than as a separate module because the heritage is the bridge between the biology and the ritual.

Format: founder talking head, narrated B-roll of the gummy, He Shou Wu botanical illustration, simple animated follicle diagram.

Downloadable: a one-page reference card with the hair growth cycle and the He Shou Wu heritage timeline.

## Module 2, Reading Your Body's Signals

**Length: 6 to 8 minutes.** Taught by Dafina.

The difference between shedding and thinning, telogen and anagen, fragility and breakage. How to tell what your hair is actually telling you, and what it isn't. This is the diagnostic empathy module. The one Dafina wishes she had watched before her first dermatology appointment.

Format: founder talking head, side-by-side photo references, no clinical cut-in needed.

Downloadable: a short field guide titled "Is it shedding or thinning."

## Module 3, The Hormonal Layer

**Length: 15 to 18 minutes.** Taught by Dafina, with clinical guidance from Dr. Miriam Safahi, MD.

Postpartum, perimenopause, GLP-1, post-pill, thyroid. The hormonal shifts that show up in your hair first, and how each one looks different. Dafina walks through what she learned in each of these chapters of her own life, and what the research says about why hair is often the first symptom anyone notices.

Format: founder talking head as the spine, three to five Safahi clinical cut-ins drawn from the existing Folicelle Safahi audio interview and the two YouTube live interviews. Cut-ins are short, sourced clips, captioned so it reads as a clinical aside, not a co-teach. Founder voice carries the through-line.

Downloadable: a one-page reference titled "Hair changes by hormonal chapter."

## Module 4, The Nutritional Layer

**Length: 15 to 18 minutes.** Taught by Dafina, with clinical guidance from Dr. Miriam Safahi, MD.

Iron, ferritin, B-vitamins, minerals. Why most hair vitamins miss the foundation. What clinical-strength dosages actually mean and why they matter. This is also where Dafina tells the ferritin-7 story directly. The framework is built around the lived experience of walking into Medi Verbier light-headed and walking out with a number she had never been told to ask for.

Format: founder talking head, the ferritin-7 personal narrative, Safahi cut-ins on absorption and the methylated B-complex, a comparison chart on therapeutic dosages versus drugstore gummies.

Downloadable: a one-page reference titled "Labs that read the nutritional layer," with ferritin, vitamin D, B-12 and folate, full thyroid panel, sex hormone panel. The same labs that appear in the Doctor Script.

## Module 5, The Stress and Sleep Layer

**Length: 5 to 7 minutes.** Taught by Dafina.

Cortisol, telogen effluvium, the three-month delay between a hard chapter and a thinning crown. Why sleep is a hair input, not a luxury. The connection between the months that broke you and the hair you are looking at now. Practical, brief, no clinical cut-in needed.

Format: founder talking head, light B-roll.

Downloadable: a one-page reference titled "The cortisol and hair cycle relationship."

## Module 6, The Environmental Layer

**Length: 5 to 7 minutes.** Taught by Dafina.

Water, heat, products, scalp microbiome, the topical layer. The factors you can change tomorrow morning, and the ones that matter less than the internet says they do. Dafina separates the noise from the signal so members stop spending money on things that will not move the needle.

The deeper treatment of heat, water filtration, and topical products lives in a quarterly bonus module (see "Quarterly bonus modules" below). Module 6 is the short overview. Members who want the deeper version know where to find it.

Format: founder talking head, light B-roll of shower, blow dryer, hairstylist chair.

Downloadable: a one-page "What to change first" checklist.

## Module 7, Advocating for Yourself in the Medical System

**Length: 18 to 22 minutes.** Taught by Dafina.

The module the Method is built around. Everything else exists to make this one usable. The story spine is the four practitioners who missed Dafina's ferritin, including the Harvard dermatologist, the Westport dermatologist who suggested a testosterone pellet program, the well-meaning hairstylist who suggested Rogaine, and the GP who said "you're just a mom of twins." The lesson is the script. How to walk into a GP or dermatologist's office with the right questions, the right labs, and a printable doctor script that turns a fifteen-minute appointment into a productive one.

Format: founder talking head, story-driven. Visual references to the labs to ask for. A clear walkthrough of the Doctor Script Generator, what it produces, and how to use it. No clinical cut-ins. This is Dafina's room.

Downloadable: a sample doctor script PDF. The live Doctor Script Generator inside the Toolkit produces a personalized version.

## Module 8, Your Protocol

**Length: 6 to 8 minutes.** Taught by Dafina.

Synthesizing what you have learned. How to read your own pattern. Where Foundation fits, where it doesn't, and the realistic timeline for visible results, which for most women is three to six months of consistent practice. Members complete the Course-Completion Re-assessment inside Module 8, which generates an updated personalized protocol inside the Toolkit. The certificate of completion issues automatically once Module 8 is marked viewed and the re-assessment is submitted.

Format: founder talking head, closing tone, direct address to the member.

Downloadable: the member's updated personalized protocol PDF, generated from the Re-assessment.

# The Hair Stage Assessment, three instances

The Assessment appears in three distinct surfaces. The instrument is the same shape, the depth and routing differ.

**Instance 1, Public Hair Stage Assessment.** Lives at `folicelle.com/assessment`. 60 to 90 seconds. Eight questions. Cold-traffic entry point. Routes the visitor to the matching protocol landing page, then to Foundation or to the Method. No login required.

**Instance 2, In-Course Baseline Assessment.** Lives inside Tevello, gates Module 1 unlock. Roughly 3 to 5 minutes. Deeper than the public version. Captures stage, current regimen, prior labs if any, primary symptoms, medical history relevant to hair, what the member wants from the year. Output: a baseline protocol, cohort assignment, and the inputs that feed the Doctor Script Generator.

**Instance 3, Course-Completion Re-assessment.** Lives inside Module 8. Roughly 3 to 5 minutes. Same instrument as the Baseline, with the addition of "what has shifted since you began." Output: an updated personalized protocol delivered as a PDF, plus the trigger that issues the certificate of completion.

The instrument lives in the Self-Advocacy Toolkit and renders inside Tevello via the customer account UI. Engineering for the in-course instance can be deferred to Lovable build phase per master handoff Section 22.

# The Doctor Script Generator

A personalized one-page request framework the member prints and brings to a GP or dermatologist appointment. Built as an AI-powered generator inside the Self-Advocacy Toolkit, similar in pattern to Nick Sharma's customized landing page approach. The model ingests:

- The member's Baseline Assessment answers.
- The labs and language referenced in the eight core modules.
- The member's stage tag (Postpartum, Perimenopause, Metabolic, Foundation).

The model outputs a one-page printable script with:

- Patient context paragraph customized to the member's situation.
- Labs to discuss, ranked by relevance to the member's stage.
- A short plain-language justification per lab.
- A notes section the member fills in during the appointment.

Build approach: Lovable prototype in v1, ported to a Shopify Customer Account UI Extension. Claude API as the model, with the prompt template versioned and held in `/method-content/prompts/`. Output legal disclaimer ("This script is educational and intended to support a conversation with your healthcare provider. It is not a diagnosis or prescription.") is non-negotiable per master handoff Section 3. Claims attorney reviews the prompt template before launch.

The static sample on `/toolkit/index.html` stays as the marketing illustration of what the generator produces. The live generator delivers the personalized version once the member is logged in.

# Community structure

The course community lives inside Tevello as the native course community feature. Access begins at course enrollment, not at completion. Members who do not finish the course remain in the community.

Four cohort subchannels at launch:

- The Postpartum Path
- The Perimenopause Path
- The Metabolic Path (GLP-1, weight changes, metabolic syndrome)
- The Foundation Path (general thinning, fine hair, hair quality)

Members are assigned to one cohort via the Baseline Assessment, can opt into a second cohort if they sit between stages. A general cross-cohort channel exists for kickoff week, quarterly check-ins, and the December reflection.

Dafina presence in the community: live during kickoff week (January) and during the December reflection. Otherwise async, light touch, no scheduled office hours. Community moderator carries the day-to-day per master handoff Section 5, with Safahi reviewing flagged medical content weekly.

# Quarterly bonus modules

Three bonus drops per year. Each is roughly 8 to 12 minutes, founder-taught, designed to deepen one specific layer of the framework. Bonus modules accumulate for life. Every customer gets every future addition. No upgrade required.

**April drop, Year 1: The Heat, Water, and Topicals Deep Dive.** The fuller treatment of what Module 6 introduces. Water filtration, heat tools, shampoo formulation honesty, scalp oils, peptide serums, what to keep and what to drop.

**July drop, Year 1: The HRT, GLP-1, and Hair Loss Specifics Module.** Optional bonus. For members on hormone replacement therapy, GLP-1 medications, or both. What the research says about hair changes on these therapies. Practical adjustments. Reviewed by Dr. Safahi before release.

**October drop, Year 1: The Hairstylist Relationship Module.** Optional. How to talk to your stylist about a wellness shift. When to ask for input. What stylists can see that doctors miss. Maps cleanly to the sister-brand narrative with Sitting Pretty.

Subsequent years add to this library. Candidate Year 2 drops: Post-Thyroid, Post-Pill, Pregnancy and Postpartum Deep Dive, Iron Absorption Deep Dive. To be confirmed annually.

# Quarterly check-ins, between bonus drops

April, July, October check-ins are async and community-driven. Not live. Member experience:

1. Scheduled email arrives roughly one week before each quarterly bonus drop, prompting the member to take stock.
2. The member uploads a photo to the Photo Tracker inside the Self-Advocacy Toolkit. Three angles. Stays private by default.
3. The member writes a short journal entry inside the Toolkit. Prompt: "What has shifted, what hasn't, what are you carrying into the next quarter." Free text. Optionally shareable to the cohort.
4. The community check-in thread opens for the cohort. Members check in with each other. Dafina does not need to be present.
5. The bonus module drops at the end of the check-in week.

No live event, no scheduled office hours, no founder presence beyond a one-time welcome message inside each cohort thread.

# The December reflection

One live evening, hosted by Dafina, late December (target: a Wednesday or Thursday between December 9 and December 18). Roughly 45 to 60 minutes. Held on Zoom, streamed into the community via embedded video. Recording stored in the course for members who miss it.

Content: a reflection on the year, what the cohort learned, themes that came up in the journal prompts and community threads, three to five member stories shared with permission, a preview of what is coming in Year 2 (new bonus modules, new cohort guests, any product updates). The reflection closes the year and seeds the January kickoff.

Year 1 only is live. From Year 2 onward, Dafina can decide whether to make the December reflection live again or convert to a recorded "Year in Review" module.

# Annual reset, January Year 2

Every January, every member resets into Year 2. Subscribers continue paying. Standalone $79 holders retain lifetime access. The eight core modules remain available with any updates Dafina has recorded over the prior year. The accumulated bonus modules from Year 1 (April, July, October drops) remain in the library. The kickoff week structure repeats. The Baseline Assessment is offered again as a "Year 2 snapshot" for members who want to retake it. Community cohorts reset, members can stay in their original cohort or move.

The Method is designed to evolve with the member. The framework is consistent, the context shifts.
