# Context for the editor and the producer

The December Reflection is held on a Thursday evening in early December, live on Zoom. The Zoom is streamed into the Tevello community as embedded video. Members who attend live join from their own homes. Members who cannot attend get the recording within forty-eight hours.

Year 1 only is live. Future years may convert this to a pre-recorded "Year in Review" lesson if the live cadence becomes a bandwidth problem. For Year 1, it is live and intimate and small.

The event has four movements: opening monologue from Dafina (10 minutes), member stories (15 to 20 minutes, prepared in advance with members), Year 2 preview (5 minutes), and open Q&A (10 to 20 minutes). This script covers the first and third movements. The second movement is a live segment with members; the fourth is unscripted.

---

# Movement 1, Opening monologue (~10 minutes)

## Cold open (~1 minute)

**[Sitting in a chair. Candle on the side table being lit. Camera at eye level. Direct address to the Zoom camera.]**

Hello. I am so glad we are all here.

If you remember, we lit a candle on a Friday night in January, eleven months ago, when this year started. I wanted to light another one tonight. It is the same kind of candle. It does not solve anything. It just marks that we are doing something on purpose.

This is the December reflection. Year One. We are going to spend the next hour together. The first ten minutes is mine. The middle part is going to be three members of our cohort, who agreed to share what their year actually looked like. The end is a Q&A, where anything is on the table.

Before we go any further, thank you. For showing up. For staying. For being patient with your body and with mine. This year was real.

*Lower third: "Year One Reflection. December 2026."*

---

## Segment 1, What we learned about timing (~2 minutes)

I want to start by saying out loud what the Method asked of you and what it could not promise.

The Method asked you to believe that hair runs on a slower timeline than the rest of your life. To take baseline photos in January. To not check the mirror obsessively in February. To run the labs in March. To trust the year. Most of you did this.

What the Method could not promise was a result by April. Some of you noticed nothing by April. Some of you noticed shed slowing down by May. Some of you got your labs back in March and had a real conversation with a practitioner for the first time in your adult life. Some of you took a baseline photo in January, did not look at it again, and only just looked at it last week.

If you looked last week, you saw something. I have been hearing it from the cohort threads for the last three weeks. The photo from January looked back at you. The part is different. The crown is different. The energy on your face is different. The reasons are layered. Sleep. Iron. The honest practitioner you finally found. The medication you stopped. The supplement you stayed consistent with. The conversation in the cohort that gave you a sentence for what was happening.

That layered effect is the Method. It is not one input. It is the year of attention.

---

## Segment 2, What I learned this year (~2.5 minutes)

I want to be honest with you about my own year.

I went into this year telling you that ferritin was my through-line. It still is. But the deepest shift for me this year was not nutritional. It was paying attention to the consistency of my sleep window. Same bedtime. Same wake time. The boring discipline. My hair did not immediately change because of it. My energy did. The energy changed how I showed up, which changed how my body recovered, which changed everything downstream.

The second thing I learned is that I am not finished. I am two years into a slow rebuild that is still rebuilding. The cul-de-sacs at my temples that I named in the founding essay have filled in some, not all the way. The hair I see in the mirror today is real, and it is not the hair I will have in another year if I keep doing the work. The Method is not a destination. It is a practice.

The third thing I learned is from you. Specifically, from the women in the cohort threads who were honest in places where they could have been performative. Who said "nothing has changed yet and I am tired" and got held by the cohort instead of being told to try harder. Who shared their labs even when the numbers were uncomfortable. Who reported back from doctor appointments where the practitioner read the script and ran the labs, and from appointments where the practitioner did not. Both stories were useful. The honest sentence is what makes the Cohort the Cohort.

I learned that the Method is not me. It is the framework I built and the women who decided to walk through it.

---

## Segment 3, A small announcement about Year 2 (~2 minutes)

Year 2 starts in January. I want to tell you what is the same and what is new.

What is the same. The eight core modules. The toolkit. The community. The annual rhythm of kickoff, three quarterly check-ins, and this reflection. The same patience. The same framework.

What is new. Three new bonus modules will drop across the year, at the same April, July, and October cadence. The topics for Year 2 are post-thyroid, the iron absorption deep dive, and the pregnancy and postpartum extended module. The three Year 1 bonuses (heat-water-topicals, HRT and GLP-1, hairstylist relationship) remain in the library for everyone, including new members joining in Year 2.

The Cohort is open to returning members at no additional cost. If you are on a Foundation subscription, the Method renews with you. If you bought the standalone $79 Method in Year 1, your access is for life and your community access continues as long as your subscription or membership is active.

If you want a clean break and to come back later, that is fine too. The Method will be here.

I am keeping the cadence of Dafina-in-the-community light again next year. Kickoff in January, three quarterly check-in videos, this reflection in December. The cohort runs because of the moderator, the members, and the rhythm. That is by design.

---

## Segment 4, A short personal close (~2 minutes)

I built Folicelle because four practitioners missed my ferritin and I had to fly to Switzerland to find one who did not. The Method is the version of that appointment that I wanted every woman to have without the flight.

I will keep building it. I will keep bringing in clinicians who do the work the rest of the system is not yet structured to do. The podcast in Year 2 will be one of the ways we expand who you hear from. Foundation will keep being the daily ritual for those of you it serves.

What I want you to know, before the next part of tonight starts, is that the framework is yours forever. The hair will keep doing what hair does, on its own slow timeline. Your knowledge of how to read it will not regress.

That is the gift of having watched. You cannot unsee it.

Now I want to bring in the first member story. Sarah, are you on with us?

*[Live segment begins. Dafina hands over to Member 1. Members 1, 2, and 3 each share a 3 to 5 minute story, prepared in advance with cohort moderator. Stories are framed by Dafina as a host, with one-question follow-ups. Recording captures the full segment for the post-event posting, with member permission.]*
