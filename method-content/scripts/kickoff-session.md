# Cold open (~30 seconds)

**[On camera. Sitting, not standing. Camera at eye level. Direct address.]**

Hi. I am so glad you are here.

If you are watching this on the Friday night of January kickoff week, you are probably sitting somewhere with a glass of wine or a cup of tea and you have already had a long week. I have too. I am going to keep this short.

The Method is a year, not a thirty-day program. We are going to spend the next twelve months together. Tonight is the beginning.

*B-roll suggestion: open with a tight shot of a candle being lit, then pull back to reveal Dafina sitting in the chair. Title card simple: "The Folicelle Method, Year One Kickoff."*

# Segment 1, Why this year (~2 minutes)

I want to tell you what the Method is, and what it is not.

The Method is not a program that you finish. It is not eight modules you watch in a row and then move on with your life. It is not a thirty-day reset. It is not a hair fix.

The Method is a framework. Eight modules that teach you how to read what your body is telling you through your hair. A Toolkit that helps you turn that reading into a conversation you can have with a practitioner. A community of women who are working through the same thing. A handful of bonus modules that come throughout the year. And one evening in December where we sit down together and look back at what shifted.

The reason the Method is a year is that hair is a year. A single hair cycle is somewhere between three and six months. The changes you see in your hair this April will be a delayed signal of choices you started making this January. The changes you see in December will be the layered effect of a full year of inputs. If you watched a thirty-day course, you would not see anything yet. We are going to give your body the time it needs.

I want you to know, before we go any further, that you are allowed to move through this at your pace. Some of you are going to watch all eight modules in the first weekend. Some of you are going to start Module 1 in February. Some of you are going to skip around. All of that is fine. The Method is built to wait.

*B-roll suggestion: a calendar with January 1 visible, a hand turning the page slowly. Cut back to Dafina.*

# Segment 2, What this week looks like (~2 minutes)

Here is what is happening this week.

All eight core modules are unlocked. They are sitting in your account waiting for you. The order they are in is the order I would suggest, but the modules are deliberately self-contained. If Module 4 on nutrition is the one you most need this week, start with Module 4.

Module 7, which is the advocacy module and the doctor script module, is the one I built the Method around. If you are in the kind of moment where you need to walk into an appointment soon, watch Module 7 first. Print your script. Book the appointment. The rest will catch up.

Your Toolkit is open. The Baseline Assessment you filled out before this video should have routed you to a cohort. The Postpartum Path, the Perimenopause Path, the Metabolic Path, or the Foundation Path. You can sit in more than one cohort if your situation does not fit cleanly. You can move. The cohort threads opened today. You will see a welcome from a few of us already in there.

For the rest of this week, I am going to post one short message a day in the general community. Tuesday, what the year actually looks like. Wednesday, how to use the cohorts. Thursday, how the Doctor Script Generator works. Friday, this evening, the live event. After Friday, I get quieter. The next time you see me showing up in the community at this level is the December reflection.

That is on purpose. The Method is not a personality. It is a practice. The community is the company you keep through the practice, and you are going to be the company for each other.

# Segment 3, A small thing I want to say about expectations (~2 minutes)

I want to be honest about what the next twelve months will and will not deliver.

What the Method will deliver is a framework you will keep for the rest of your life. The five labs you will learn how to ask for. The way hormones and nutrition and stress and environment all show up in your hair. The script you can hand a practitioner. The vocabulary for the conversation you have not been able to have yet.

What the Method will not deliver is a hair fix. There is no thirty-day before-and-after. If anyone is selling you that, they are selling you something else. Real hair changes are three to six months minimum, often longer, and the path is rarely linear. You will have months where you cannot tell if anything is shifting. You will have months where you suddenly catch your reflection and notice your part looks different. That is the rhythm.

I want you to be patient with yourself this year. I want you to take photos at baseline this week, even if you do not feel ready to take them. I want you to write down what you notice, not what you wish was happening. I want you to come into the cohort threads and say "I do not know if anything is changing" if that is the honest sentence, instead of the polished one.

The Method is built for the honest sentence.

*B-roll suggestion: a notebook page being written on, the words not legible but the act of writing visible. A small calendar with the next April, July, October, and December circled.*

# Segment 4, What you are committing to (~2 minutes)

Three things, this year, are the practice.

One. Watch the modules. At your pace. In your order. As many times as you want. The Method is yours forever.

Two. Run the labs. Not because labs are the Method, but because the labs are the most concrete thing the Method gives you. Walking into an appointment with the script is the one moment of the year where everything you have learned turns into something you can hold in your hand. I want every one of you to do this at least once this year. If you have run the labs already, run them again at the end of the year as a comparison.

Three. Show up to the cohort. Not every day. Not even every week. Show up when you have something to say or when you need something to hear. The cohort is what makes the Method a year instead of a binder.

And then, in December, on a Thursday evening, we will all come back together and look at what the year added up to. I will hold space for that. I will share what I have learned this year too. You will hear from a few members of this cohort whose stories will probably make you cry. We will not have figured everything out. We will have moved.

# The teaching moment (~30 seconds)

If you take only one thing from this kickoff, take this. You are not late. You are not behind. The Method is not graded. You are here at the right time, because today is the day you started.

The rest is just showing up.

# Editor notes

**Pacing.** The kickoff has to feel like an evening, not a class. Slower than the modules. Let the silences breathe.

**Tone reference.** This is closer to the C&M live with Safahi where Dafina says "let's not be locked in by the questions" (Episode 1, around 2:13). Conversational, present, unscripted-feeling, even though the bullet points are firm.

**The candle and the calendar.** Two visual motifs I would carry through: a single candle being lit at the open and blown out at the close, and a wall calendar with the year visible. Both quiet, both warm, both temporal. Nothing else. Resist the urge to add B-roll of the gummy or the Toolkit. This is not a product video.

**Existing footage to consider intercutting**: none. The kickoff is a sit-down. Do not intercut. The whole point is that Dafina is sitting in one chair, in one room, talking to the viewer like a friend.

**On-screen text overlays**:
- "You are not late." rendered as a centered lower third at the teaching moment, brand display font.
- Module names listed lower-right when she walks through the eight modules in Segment 2, brand sans, light treatment.

**Things to be careful of in editing**:
- Do not allow the kickoff to feel like an "Intro to Folicelle." It is intro to the year, not to the brand. The viewer has already bought in.
- Do not let Dafina seem rehearsed in this script. If a take feels too polished, take it again.
- Do not include a "Welcome to Folicelle" sting or any branded title card at the open. The brand is implicit. The candle is the title card.

**Runtime estimate at first read**: roughly 9 minutes 30 seconds. Inside the 8-to-12 target range. The live event extends this with Q&A, but the recorded version posted to the course should hold close to this length.
