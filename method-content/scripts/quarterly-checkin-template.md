# How to use this template

Three short check-in videos per year. Each one drops with the corresponding quarterly bonus module:

| Check-in | Drops with | Bonus module that month (Year 1) | Season variable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 Check-in | April | The Heat, Water, and Topicals Deep Dive | Spring, the noticing month |
| Q2 Check-in | July | The HRT, GLP-1, and Hair Loss Specifics Module | Summer, the slow month |
| Q3 Check-in | October | The Hairstylist Relationship Module | Fall, the returning month |

The video is the same shape every time. The variables are season, what to ask the cohort to do, and one specific reflection question. Filming these can happen in batch: one shoot day in the backyard captures all three with wardrobe changes and seasonal B-roll cutaways added in post.

# Cold open (~15 seconds)

**[On camera, casual setting, no chair, often standing or walking. Lower production register than the modules.]**

Hi. It is [SEASON] check-in time. I am [LOCATION: in my backyard / on a walk / sitting at the kitchen counter]. I want to spend three minutes with you before the [MONTH] bonus drops.

*B-roll suggestion: seasonal exterior shot opening the video (spring buds, summer sun, fall leaves). Title card simple: "Q[N] Check-In, [MONTH]."*

# Segment 1, The same three things, every quarter (~1.5 minutes)

I am going to ask you to do three things this week. The same three things I ask every quarter. The repetition is the point.

One. Open the Photo Tracker. Take your three angles. Part, crown, full back. Compare them to the photos from the last check-in. Compare them to your baseline photos from January. Notice what you notice. The Toolkit will show them to you side by side. The point is not whether your hair looks better. The point is whether you can see the year as data instead of as feeling.

Two. Write a short journal entry inside the Toolkit. The prompt is the same every quarter, with one variable: [QUARTER-SPECIFIC QUESTION]. For Q1, that question is "what did you change in the first three months." For Q2, it is "what slowed down or stopped between April and July." For Q3, it is "what feels different about your hair, your body, or your attention this fall." Free text. As much or as little as you want. The journal stays private. You can share to the cohort if you want.

Three. Show up in your cohort thread this week. Read what other members posted. Reply to one or two. The cohort gets quiet in the months between check-ins, and the check-in is what wakes it back up. Be a person who shows up for the cohort, and the cohort will be a person who shows up for you.

That is it. Twenty minutes of total work. The framework is asking you to do exactly this much, no more.

**[Variable per quarter. The personal observation should be a sentence about Dafina's own framework progress, not a brand statement. Examples:]**

*For Q1 April*: "Honestly, the thing that has changed the most for me since January is sleep. The bedtime hour I committed to in Module 5 has been the lever. I did not expect that. Three months in, my hair has not visibly changed yet. My energy has. The Method runs on the long timeline, and I am inside it with you."

*For Q2 July*: "We are in the slow month. I am not pushing you to be productive about your hair right now. It is summer. Travel. Show up loosely. The Method is not going anywhere. I will see you in October."

*For Q3 October*: "Coming back from summer is its own moment. The Method is a year, and we are entering the last third. The October bonus on stylists is one of my favorite ones. Watch it before your next salon appointment if you can."

Lock the actual line in the shoot, off-the-cuff. The bullet points above are starting frames, not script.

# Editor notes

**Tone.** These are the lowest-production register of the entire Method. The check-ins should feel like a friend FaceTiming you. Tight crop, natural light, minimal cut. Two-minute videos do not need motion graphics or title sequences.

**Batch filming.** All three can be shot on a single day if scheduled across the seasons (impossible) OR if filmed seasonally on three different days throughout the year. Recommend three separate days, each in the right month, so the season is honest in frame.

**Existing footage**: none. The check-ins are present-moment.

**On-screen text overlays**:
- Date and quarter as a clean lower third at the open
- The three actions ("Photos, Journal, Cohort Visit") as a stack in Segment 1
- Bonus module name as a lower third in Segment 2

**Things to be careful of**:
- Do not film all three check-ins at once and pretend they are seasonal. The audience will read it. Honor the seasons.
- Do not let any check-in run longer than three minutes. The whole point is brevity. If a take goes long, cut.

**Runtime estimate**: each video runs roughly 2 minutes 30 seconds. Three videos per year. Combined runtime under 10 minutes.
