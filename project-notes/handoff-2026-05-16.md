## 3. Tone of voice rules

- No em-dashes. Use periods, commas, semicolons, or rephrase. Hard rule for every piece of new copy.
- First-person from Dafina. "I" not "we." "I learned" not "the Method teaches."
- Natural prose rhythm. Vary sentence length. No constructed AI cadence (no triplet sentences, no fake-list parallelism).
- FTC-safe language per master handoff Section 3: supports, nourishes, may help, associated with. Never treats, cures, prevents, reverses, combats, stops.

There is no standalone tone-of-voice file in the repo yet. Voice is currently defined by the merged copy on `method.html`, `index.html`, and `product/foundation.html`. Use those as ground truth.
